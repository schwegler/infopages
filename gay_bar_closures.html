Reference notes & original examples

The existing project reports a 37% decline in U.S. gay-bar listings from 2002 to 2019 and attributes its framing to sociologist Greggor Mattson. The primary study has not been independently reconciled in this redesign. Listings are not a census of every operating venue.

Decline by demographic

Compare the demographic views. The original reported declines are 37% for general male-oriented bars, 52% for women’s bars and 60% for bars serving people of color. Selecting a group reorders the comparison; it does not change the source values.
